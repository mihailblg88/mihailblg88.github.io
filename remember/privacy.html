Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

I Want to Remember! is a memory assistant: you tell it where you put things, and later ask where they are. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Your notes

  • Notes are stored only on your device. The developer has no server and never receives them.
  • If Android backup is turned on, Android may include app data in your Google account backup, as with any app.
  • Deleting a note or uninstalling the app removes the notes from the device.

Microphone and speech

  • The microphone is used only while you press the microphone button.
  • Speech is converted to text by your device's speech recognition service (usually Google), which may process audio under its own privacy policy. The app does not record or store audio.
  • Answers are spoken by your device's text-to-speech engine.

Advertising

  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and approximate location to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps.
  • Where required by law (for example, in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads. You can change your choice with “Ad settings” at the bottom of the app.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
